🔍 Detail Skill

Single Target Make Some Noise

Deals Lightning DMG equal to #1[i]% of the Trailblazer's ATK to a single enemy.

AoE Let the Storm Rage On

Deals Lightning DMG equal to #1[i]% of the Trailblazer's ATK to all enemies and gains 20 point(s) of "Certified Banger."

Support That Smile Hits Different

After using an attack, regenerates a fixed amount of 10 Energy and gains 3 Punchline point(s). When the Trailblazer holds "Certified Banger," their Skill additionally deals #1[i]% Lightning Elation DMG to all enemies. This DMG is calculated using the highest "Certified Banger" value among all allies.

Enhance Immortal Third Strike

After using Technique, 1 of the following effects will be randomly granted: Small chance to gain "Hearty Laughter": Increases Elation by 30%. High chance to gain "Irrepressible Laughter": Increases Elation by 20%. When the next battle begins, increases all allies' Elation stat by the corresponding amount for 3 turn(s).

AoE I Said "Elation," Did I Stutter?

Deals 8 instances of DMG, with each instance dealing #1[i]% Lightning Elation DMG to one random enemy. Then, deals #2[i]% Lightning Elation DMG, which is split evenly among all enemies.
